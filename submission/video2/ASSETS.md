# Sakshi explainer: assets, method and provenance

**Final file:** `sakshi_explainer.mp4`, 1920×1080, 30 fps, H.264 + AAC 48 kHz stereo, English captions burned in and as a soft track (`sakshi_explainer.srt`).

## Method: motion-graphics-skills (charlie947/motion-graphics-skills, MIT)
Built following the pack's `vox-explainer`, `launch-video` and `motion-effects` skill instructions:
- **One self-contained film** (`film.html`) with **`window.seek(seconds)`** that draws the exact frame for any time; no free-running animations or unseeded randomness. Rendered by stepping `seek()` at 30 fps and encoding to MP4 (`render-frames.mjs`), which is the export route the skills allow ("any tool that steps through window.seek and saves frames as MP4").
- **One script line per shot** (11 shots), with the question first and a five-word takeaway last ("The truth leaves a trace.").
- **Premium = one object changing shape**, with staggered entrances, springs (not linear easing), ghosts of the start state (struck-through old hash; "ignored" witness cards), cards moving in depth, and an accent wipe on every cut.
- **Sound written in code** (`build-audio.mjs`): a low pad bed, a soft hit on every cut, a lift into the last shot, ducked under the voice.
- **Every fact on screen has a source:** hashes and entity keys (`../video/cli_output.txt`, `arkiv/schema.md`), wallet addresses and transactions (`docs/SUBMISSION.md`), and "India blocked the Wayback Machine, Aug 2017" (The Wire, Aug 2017). The WhatsApp chat in shot 1 is labelled "illustration".
- **Brand:** the live site's palette and type (paper #F6F4F2, navy #172344, vermillion #E4502F; Geist, Geist Mono, Newsreader).
